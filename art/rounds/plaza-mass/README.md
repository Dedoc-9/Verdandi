# Round: "make the towers read as one monumental mass, not a comb"

The second round of DIRECTOR-0 on the plaza-night brief. It answers the art note left after the first round's
candidate A: one prism to a cell, each at its own height, reads as a comb of spikes rather than one brutalist mass.
The candidates use the exporter's `mass` option, recorded in the ledger by a rebase (#14), and were written at head
`cac46b5c8e28`. The verdicts are the ledger's (events 15 to 17), not this page's.

| file | the idea | scope | verdict at that head |
|---|---|---|---|
| `A.intent` | the plaza's ring as a few masses, stepped from 30 m at the middle of each side to 16 m at the corners, each with a cornice | `plaza/tower/*` | CLEAN |
| `B.intent` | as A, and the two bases' low towers massed the same way | `plaza/tower/*`, `base-a/tower/*`, `base-b/tower/*` | CLEAN |
| `C.intent` | as A, pushed from 20 to 36 m, the crown over the north slot clear of the skyline | `plaza/tower/*` | CLEAN |

Nothing here is admitted until the owner chooses. Seen in the native renderer, the head reads as a comb from above,
and A, B and C read as stepped blocks with cornices. C's crown is the strongest. B changes little that can be seen
from the plaza.
